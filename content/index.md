Hey! I'm Vihaan. 

You've stumbled across the very messy forest of my notes. They're pretty weird. Some notes address an imaginary reader, others address me, and still others are statements and don't address anybody in particular. They aren't terrifically public-facing, but I do (occasionally) try to keep an audience in mind.  

These notes are generally on topics that interest me, so knowing more about me would probably help you get a grasp of the types of notes I would right about. 

Right now, I'm fascinated by [[What my tft research should be|building tools that can augment cognition]], taking AI/ML-adjacent courses, [history](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-rest-is-history/id1537788786), and competitive programming puzzles. Within building tools that augment cognition, I'm especially interested in [[Making Massive Input Better|information retrieval]]. Over the next few years (once I start figuring out what I really want to do with my life), I also want to build the kind of [[Social Infrastructure]] for others that's been missing for a lot of my life (and in that vein I'm also interested in wider [[What my ideal high school looks like|education reform]]). (Sidenote: I'm generally *super* interested in one thing at a time with tons of side interests, and I'm constantly looking for cool things that I might want to work on more deeply after high school. Let me know if there's an underrated field that you love and wish more people worked on.) If you want to know more about why I'm interested in these things, see [[A Brief Autobiography]].
