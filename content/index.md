Hey! I'm Vihaan. 

You've stumbled across the very messy forest of my notes. They're pretty weird. Some notes address an imaginary reader, others address me, and still others are statements and don't address anybody in particular. They aren't terrifically public-facing, but I do (occasionally) try to keep an audience in mind.  

I think a lot about topics that interest me, so knowing more about me would probably help you get a grasp of the types of notes I would write about. 

Right now, I'm fascinated by AI/ML-adjacent courses, [history](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-rest-is-history/id1537788786), competitive programming puzzles, and building prototypes of [[What my tft research should be|tools that can augment cognition]] (specifically [[Making Massive Input Better|information retrieval]], [[Search of the future|search]], and [[Future Operating Systems|future operating systems]]). Over the next few years (once I start figuring out what I really want to do with my life), I also want to build the kind of [[Social Infrastructure]] for others that's been missing for a lot of my life (and in that vein I'm also interested in wider [[What my ideal high school looks like|education reform]]). I'm also a passionate user of spaced repetition systems, and I use them to do [[Anki Uses|interesting things]] like [[How to change the way you think|changing the way I think]] and [[You can change your identity|changing the way I see myself]]. 

See [[Open Questions + Project Ideas]] for questions to think about and projects I'd love people to work. If you want to know more about why I'm interested in the things I mentioned, see [[A Brief Autobiography]]. Visit [[Why this exists|this note]] to learn more about why these notes even exist. Visit [[Paths to get started|this note]] for potential paths to get started. Visit [[Things I Love|this note]] for more about me.
