Conversations with the right people serve as breeding grounds for [[Idea Triggers]]. 

I see two main reasons for this:
1. The best conversations are with people who are similar enough that you have common conversation topics and a shared "canon", but different enough that they can be good idea triggers.
2. Talking about your ideas forces you to spend more time with them than when they're a casual thought in your head.
