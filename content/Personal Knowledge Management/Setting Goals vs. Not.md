This is something that I've been thinking about a lot recently. 

As of now, I've resolved to only have long-term goals as guides for roughly where I want to go and short-term ones only insofar as they boost my daily/weekly productivity. You can't predict the future, and sticking religiously to a long-term goal only creates this artificial pressure that doesn't need to exist. A long-term plan will almost always fail, and you might not even have the same values by the time it "succeeds". 

I think that if I use doing [[Excitingly Ambitious Things]] as my north star I'll live my best and most authentic life.
