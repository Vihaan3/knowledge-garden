Note from [[Thinking, Fast and Slow]]

2 separate results about cognitive ease combine to be troubling:
1. When you are at cognitive ease, your System 2 is less likely to be activated. 
2. Familiar things put you at ease. 

##### Interesting Results 
This is why it's so easy to have [[Trapped Priors]].

This also might be why I [[On the importance of friction|prefer friction]] when it comes to tasks I highly value.

You can become more convincing by promoting cognitive ease:
- Readable font
- Low-strain vocabulary
- High contrast

Also interesting: there is some evidence to suggest that creativity comes when you are at cognitive ease. If your System 2 being activated is loosely considered "problem-solving", this suggests a tension between problem-solving and creativity, which makes me think that there might be more to [[Two Types of Thinking]] than I'd previously thought.
