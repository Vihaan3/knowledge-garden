#pattern #personal 

True understanding that you can just do something. It's hard to really describe well, but Neel Nanda does a [good job](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/44-agency).

When stated like this, it feels pretty obvious, but I think most people don't realize that they can *just go and do something.* There's nothing insanely different about the people who do the thing that you want to but don't think you can. And if there is something different, you can change yourself. 

Examples of agency:
- [[You can change your identity]]
- You can start a company
- You can give yourself more [[Slack]]
- You can become "smarter"
- You can start the biggest club in your school
- You can be happier
- You can cultivate better friendships
- You can go and email somebody awesome
- You can think deeply about your life choices and decide not to take the default path
