This is a massive pet peeve of mine. Some of the smartest people I know have this idea that once we [[You can't solve alignment|"solve" alignment]], we'll proceed to live in a perfect utopia of human flourishing. 

My biggest quibble with this is that, [advancement's in knowledge are almost always dual use](https://michaelnotebook.com/xrisk/). I think it's highly unlikely that there's a summit to reach where everything will be perfect forever. As we gain more knowledge and understanding, we increase our general capabilities to influence the world and I don't think you can reliably separate the positive from the negative. The most you can do is try to suppress the negative and amplify the positive as much as possible.
