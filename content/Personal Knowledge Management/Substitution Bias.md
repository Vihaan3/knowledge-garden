The mother of many cognitive biases. This is the tendency to substitute a hard question for an easier question, often without even recognizing it.  

Examples:
- How much would I pay to save 1000 birds from an oil spill? -> What do I feel when I see the image of a dying bird?
- How happy am I with my life? -> How have I been feeling over the last few hours?
