#### Lecture 1: First Five Lessons

Strictly dominate: Strategy alpha strictly dominates strategy beta when the payoff from alpha is strictly greater than that of beta *regardless of what others do*.

Lesson 1: Never play a strictly dominated game.
Lesson 2: Rational play can lead to bad outcomes.
Lesson 3: To decide what action to choose, figure out what your payoffs are and what the payoffs of the other players are. 
Lesson 4: Put yourself in your opponent's shoes.
Lesson 5: Yale students are evil :). 

My prediction for the last game: 18. 
Rationale: You should always be 2/3 of everybody else. But everyone knows that you'll try to be 2/3 of them, so they'll go down by a third as well. I think most people will do this 2-3 times and then stop once they're satisfied that they're ahead of everyone else.

Ingredients of a game:
- players
- strategies
- payoffs

Notation:
- players: *i*
- strategies: 
	- Set of all possible strategies of player *i*: S (subscript)i
	- A particular strategy of player *i*: s (subscript)i
	- A particular play of the game: s
- payoffs: 
	- U (subscript)i
