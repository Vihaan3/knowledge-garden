Echoes from Tolkien's life
- Nazgul and the omnipresent threat in the air
- Council of Elrond 
- How far can you go in trying to defeat something evil before you turn evil (using the ring against Sauron)
- Interesting head-canon from me: it seems like the description of Frodo after being stabbed by the Morgul-knife echoes Tolkien's own experiences being ill.
- Tolkien grows up in an idyllic place that was always being encroached on by industrialization
- Beren and Luthien
- Ideas about intense male friendship and camaraderie probably come from his experiences at school
