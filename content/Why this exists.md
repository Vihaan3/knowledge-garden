I have tons of random thoughts, and I like [translating those ideas to words](https://paulgraham.com/words.html) in a physical journal to [help them accumulate](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_note-writing_helps_insight_accumulate). To make that more public, I started a [blog](https://vihaansondhi.substack.com/), but blogging is pretty time-consuming, so I only publish the most interesting stuff there. 

Inspired by [Andy Matuschak](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/About_these_notes), [Jacky Zhao](https://jzhao.xyz/), and [Jess Martin](https://notes.jessmart.in/Lab+Notebook/About+this+lab+notebook), I made this the spot for everything that falls in between my journal and the blog. The hope is that this will add some pressure on me to keep these notes (relatively) legible and [Evergreen](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_notes).
