I keep an infrequently updated blog at https://vihaansondhi.substack.com/, but I spend a lot of time thinking, and I like [translating those ideas to words](https://paulgraham.com/words.html) in a physical journal to [help them accumulate](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_note-writing_helps_insight_accumulate). I wanted those entries to be more [Evergreen](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_notes), so I wrote them up in Obsidian. I've recently become very aware of how life-changing meeting cool people is, and inspired by [Andy Matuschak](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/About_these_notes), [Jacky Zhao](https://jzhao.xyz/), and [Jess Martin](https://notes.jessmart.in/Lab+Notebook/About+this+lab+notebook), I began pushing those Obsidian notes to the internet. The hope is that this will [[Serendipity Maximizers|expand my serendipity]] and add some pressure on me to keep these notes relatively legible.
