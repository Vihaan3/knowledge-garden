#note-taking #tools_for_thought 
Reference: https://thesephist.com/posts/inc/

Linus believes that a promising direction for note-taking is append-only, and centered around a focus on time (like a journal or Alexander Obenauer's [Ollos](https://alexanderobenauer.com/ollos/)). 

Ideally, it basically grows with you, and is a true history of not only your present situation like other note-taking tools but how you got there. 

>"Regardless of how you recall information back from your notes, a great note-taking system should make it trivial to get ideas out, as well as in."
