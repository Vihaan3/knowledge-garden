Because ideas are [[Ideas as social graphs|heavily linked]], they come slowly into view as the right [[Idea Triggers|triggers]] strike. Many ideas will feel like they've been germinating for a while or seem really obvious in retrospect because you've been making all the preparations for the current leap of insight. [[Spaced everything]] type systems seem to be good for helping increase the speed with which they'll come into view because they serve as triggers.
