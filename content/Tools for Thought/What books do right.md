 Tools for thought culture tends to spend a lot of time focusing on [[Why Books Don't Work|the failings of books]], but I think that despite their failings, books are actually pretty good at a lot of things. They help you build [[Mental Models]] of things and serve as pseudo-[[Enacted Experiences]]. They're not necessarily good for helping your remember things, which is something that could be worked on, but I think that other than that they're pretty good. 

Paul Graham: "Your mind is like a compiled program you've lost the source of. It works, but you don't know why." https://paulgraham.com/know.html
